# Sources used to build the simulator

The model is a teaching synthesis. Cell identities, polarity rules and tissue order follow these references.

## Core textbooks

1. Esau, K. *Plant Anatomy.* 2nd / 3rd ed. Wiley.  
   Primary source for tissue systems, meristems, xylem and phloem cell types, endodermis, and secondary growth.

2. Evert, R. F. *Esau’s Plant Anatomy: Meristems, Cells, and Tissues of the Plant Body.* 3rd ed. Wiley, 2006.  
   Updated treatment of cambial initials, sieve elements, and wall modifications.

3. Raven, P. H., Evert, R. F., Eichhorn, S. E. *Biology of Plants.* (multiple editions). W. H. Freeman.  
   Organ-level organisation, SAM/RAM, secondary growth narrative.

4. Taiz, L., Zeiger, E., et al. *Plant Physiology and Development.*  
   Stomatal mechanics, Münch mass flow, root radial transport.

5. Panshin, A. J. & de Zeeuw, C. *Textbook of Wood Technology.*  
   Hardwood axial vs radial systems, vessels, fibres, rays, sapwood/heartwood.

6. NCERT Class XI, Chapter 6 *Anatomy of Flowering Plants.*  
   Standard teaching sequence: meristems, simple/complex tissues, dicot stem, root and dorsiventral leaf; endarch vs exarch.

7. Carlquist, S. *Comparative Wood Anatomy.* Springer.  
   Vessel-element evolution, perforation plates, parenchyma patterns.

8. Larson, P. R. *The Vascular Cambium.* Springer, 1994.  
   Fusiform vs ray initials; periclinal vs anticlinal divisions.

## Rules taken as standard (and shown in the model)

- Three tissue systems: dermal, ground, vascular.
- Primary meristems: protoderm, ground meristem, procambium.
- Stem primary xylem **endarch**; root primary xylem **exarch**.
- Vascular cambium produces **secondary xylem inward** and **secondary phloem outward**.
- Cambium initials: **fusiform** (axial system) and **ray** (radial system).
- Angiosperm xylem conducting cells: vessel elements + tracheids; support: fibres.
- Angiosperm phloem conducting cells: sieve-tube elements + companion cells.
- Endodermis Casparian strip blocks the apoplast into the stele.
- Pericycle is the seat of lateral-root initiation.
- Bifacial leaf: palisade adaxial, spongy + most stomata abaxial; vein xylem adaxial, phloem abaxial.
- Sapwood conducts; heartwood is chemically modified and typically non-conducting.
